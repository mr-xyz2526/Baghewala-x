## Architectural References

The following public repositories were used **only as architectural inspiration** (no code was copied). They informed design decisions such as:

- **Multi‑well view** – handling multiple wells in a single UI/dashboard.
- **Hybrid optimization** – combining heuristic search with gradient‑based refinement.
- **Economic objective** – cash‑flow and NPV calculations for well operations.
- **3D/digital‑twin visualization** – ideas for rendering well geometry and simulation results.
- **Anomaly/risk display** – visual cues for outliers and risk scores.
- **Run history** – persisting and replaying simulation runs.
- **Simulator + ML separation** – clean separation between physics‑based twin and data‑driven models.
